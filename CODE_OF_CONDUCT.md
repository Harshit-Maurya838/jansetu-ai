# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful to teammates, reviewers, faculty, partner organisations and the communities we serve.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or demeaning language, in English, Hindi or any other language.
- **Beneficiary privacy is part of conduct.** Never share real names, photos, phone numbers or health details of
  children, guardians or elderly beneficiaries in issues, PRs, chats, screenshots or demo videos. Use made-up test data.
- Field visits: follow the instructions of the Anganwadi supervisor / NGO staff, and ask before photographing anyone or anything.
